About

My research focuses on making small language models (SLMs) a cost-efficient alternative to large ones. I work across the full training pipeline, including pre-training, post-training, and reinforcement learning, with a particular focus on building environments, curating data, and designing evaluations and benchmarks.

Before my PhD, I was a software engineer on the Ads Billing team at DoorDash.
